-International Events-

Middle East: The USS Theodore Roosevelt CSG arrived in theater over the weekend, and there are now a total of three aircraft carriers within CENTCOM. Adding in the Expeditionary Sea Base, and the two Amphibious Ready Groups already in theater, there are now a total of six flattops within CENTCOM, all of which are dedicated to supporting the war in Iran.

Yemen: Over the weekend, Houthi forces continued their southern advance to the Gulf of Aden, with early Houthi advance parties arriving at the beach to the east of Aden last night. Ansar Allah (the Houthis) is expected to begin consolidating and closing the Aden pocket over the next week.

Analyst Comment: Depicting the situation on the ground is challenging as the battle lines are not as rigid as implied on the maps. The front line is porous, and large groups of Yemeni governmental forces will probably be able to escape to the east to avoid being trapped in the pocket along the southern coast.

-HomeFront-

USA: Around the nation, a major banking outage was reported this afternoon, with many banks going offline and customers being unable to access their account information. Chase, Wells Fargo, Bank of America, Capital One, Truist, USAA, Citibank, TD Bank, Huntington, American Express, and Ally were all heavily impacted by the banking outage.

Analyst Comment: The true scale of the outage/issue is not known, however with this many banks being potentially affected all at once, this is quite a serious event. Some customers reported logging in to their accounts to see a zero balance, while many others reported no observable issues. Chase bank stated that users funds were secure, but that transactions were taking longer to go through than normal due to a bug in their systems. More information on the nature of the outage is expected once services return to normal.

Illinois: The investigation continues into the IED threats that came to light over the weekend in Peoria. At least one confirmed Improvised Explosive Device (IED) was discovered at the BioUrja Renewables ethanol production facility on Friday, with mass evacuations being carried out early Saturday morning. This morning local authorities stated that they had a person of interest in custody related to the investigation, but this statement was retracted this afternoon. No further details have been released regarding this incident.
