-International Events-

Ukraine/Russia: Overnight the war in Ukraine continued to escalate, as Russia's winter offensive is well underway. Following weeks of increased Russian strikes on Kiev and outlying cities, Ukraine responded with their own large-scale drone offensive last night, targeting the Kerch Bridge yesterday evening. At least one span of the bridge collapsed after being struck by a Ukrainian cruise missile, with that section remaining out of commission. Heavy drone attacks were also reported at Russian oil and gas refineries, though the success and severity of some of these attacks is not known yet.

Norway: This afternoon, Norway's state-sponsored energy corporation reported a cyberattack targeting their systems. Equinor, the largest natural gas supplier to Europe, stated that some of their IT systems were being attacked my malign actors this afternoon, though their energy production operations have not been affected yet. As this is a developing situation at the time of this report more details are expected this evening.

Far East: President Trump arrived in Manila this morning to kick off the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) summit, with this year's focus remaining on regional partnerships and trade deals. The brief stop in the Philippines precedes the main event tomorrow, the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit. This closed-door summit will include trilateral meetings between the United States, China, and Russia, with many high-profile speeches and talks expected to take place this week.

USA: Around the nation, this afternoon Apple iCloud, App Store, and Apple Pay experienced an outage following a software update, which briefly interrupted services for a short time. Apple representatives have stated that the incident was not due to malign action.

Washington D.C. - Last night, CNN published an article claiming that unnamed sources state that Iran is attempting to attack Washington with a radiological or chemical device. No specific details for these claims were offered, and the article itself was very vague regarding these claims.

Analyst Comment: The past few days have generated a wide array of concerns, some more valid than others. Addressing the elephant in the room first, the threat of a radiological device allegedly being planned for detonation in the U.S. is almost certainly bogus. CNN's own article does not match their headline, and in the body of the text they state that some of their own sources think it's fake. Those pushing this narrative know that every time the dreaded "nuclear" word is uttered, mass panic and fear is nearly instantaneous. Perhaps the most interesting detail is that, unlike real leaks, unnamed Pentagon officials talking to CNN about impending terror attacks apparently does not warrant any type of investigation (which indicates this leak is either completely fabricated or officially sanctioned).

Monitoring the status of the extensive fleet of various platforms designed to monitor radiological threats confirms that the threat right now is nonexistent. The three Boeing WC-135 Constant Phoenix "Nuke Sniffer" aircraft have been operating normally, and the lower-level helicopters that are part of the Aerial Measuring System (AMS) program have not been observed moving around either. As of this morning, absolutely nothing indicates that government agencies are expecting some sort of nuclear/radiological/chemical threat within the homeland. Whatever unnamed sources CNN is citing for their article, they clearly are not involved in any of the longstanding counterterrorism programs that exclusively handle these types of threats.

The only abnormal strategic moves have been made are the transition of senior White House officials (and those in the line of succession) to secure locations. SECWAR Hegseth started living on a military base a while ago, and over the past few days various other cabinet members have also begun living temporarily on secure installations for unspecified reasons. Scott Bessent, Marco Rubio, Todd Blanche, and Stephen Miller have all transitioned to temporary living quarters in the D.C. area, due to unspecified threats.

The most serious concerns that have emerged over the past few days have occurred in the cyber realm, and the nearly constant random outages have started to indicate that some form of clandestine cyber campaign is being waged. There are so many unknowns it's impossible to know which incidents are the result of malign action, but speculating a bit, it is possible that the recent wave of seemingly unrelated cyber issues stems from the major infrastructure concerns of Black October, and the follow on effects of that (maybe malign actors are now able to exploit vulnerabilities that were exposed weeks ago). Whatever the case, it will be important to understand the power of narrative shaping during a time of heightened tension...a lot of the hysteria is driven by fake information, but a lot of the recent events present genuine concerns. In the absence of being able to determine which is which, defaulting to a baseline of reasonable preparedness and situational awareness is always a good idea.
