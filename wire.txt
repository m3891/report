-HomeFront-

Washington D.C. - This morning a power outage was reported in Foggy Bottom, which impacted several government agencies. The State Department, World Bank, George Washington University, and several other entities lost power after an issue was reported at a nearby substation. Smoke was reported emitting from manhole covers within the blackout zone, and work crews were able to restore power to much of the area by this afternoon. The root cause of the outage has not yet been released.

Florida: Yesterday a catastrophic plane crash was reported in Palm Beach, after a private jet experienced an unknown issue while bound for the Bahamas. One Learjet 60 (Registration: N722BD) took off from Austin, Texas at 12:49pm local time, bound for the Bahamas. As the aircraft flew over Florida, the aircraft experienced heavy turbulence due to severe weather. Subsequent ADS-B transponder data and correlated radio traffic indicate the aircraft experienced an unknown issue which caused a course change to the south, before experiencing a radical uncontrolled nosedive over Palm Beach. The aircraft impacted the ground outside the Green Acres mobile home park on South Military Trail. Concerning casualties, all 9x individuals onboard the aircraft were killed in the crash. The identities of the passengers and crew have not yet been officially released.

Analyst Comment: So far the official cause of the crash is not known, however most immediate theories center around weather possibly being a major contributing factor. Recordings of the radio traffic from the aircraft do add context of what happened leading up to the crash, but are exceptionally disturbing and traumatizing to listen to. Before the crash, the aircraft had been navigating bad weather and the flight data indicates the aircraft passed directly through a small but very intense storm cell. This may have led to increased icing conditions which may have caused the vertical stabilizer to either break off or become a nonviable control surface. If that's what happened, this would explain the extremely rapid descent and catastrophic impact with the ground. Initial NTSB press statements have indicated that a partial breakup of the aircraft is indicated by the evidence they have so far, and the full investigation is ongoing.
