-International Events-

United Kingdom: The United States has evacuated additional aircraft from airbases in England. American refueling tankers at RAF Mildenhall were relocated to bases in Germany reportedly due to an unspecified threat, and CV-22 Osprey aircraft were also dispersed to the nearby RAF Lakenheath airbase, which itself remains at a higher force protection level, as threats to the base have been expected over the past few months.

Analyst Comment: So far, the reason for the move is claimed to be due to threats related to Iran announcing a lifting of their self-imposed missile limits, and the need for American forces to get their aircraft out of Iranian missile range. However, this is probably not the reason for the move, as the aircraft were moved to Germany...which is closer to Iran. There are thousands of aircraft stationed much closer to Iran than England, so despite the reasoning for the move not being made public, it was probably due to a more localized threat.

Spain: This morning an explosive attack was reported in Oviedo. Shortly after 7:00am local time, Police initially responded to a call of a shooting at a residential building in Villafría, where they discovered a 17-year-old female with gunshot wounds. At some point during the incident, an Improvised Explosive Device (IED) was located inside a backpack at the site of the shooting. Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) personnel moved the IED outside the building to the sidewalk, in an attempt to reduce the risk to the building residents who were still evacuating. After the IED was relocated outside the building, the device detonated during the attempt to render it safe. The resulting explosion killed one officer, and resulted in an EOD technician's leg being amputated. This afternoon, the suspect was identified as Manuel Ferrero Álvarez (alias: El Ruso), a 64-year-old resident with a local criminal record. El Ruso remains at large, with authorities remaining on scene to search the local area for his whereabouts.

Analyst Comment: In the Middle East, developments continue as both peace and war hang in the balance. For the most part, the status quo remains with regard to kinetic targeting, as Iran and the United States have continued striking merchant shipping in the Gulf and random targets on Qeshm Island, respectively. On the economic front, crude prices have fallen sharply on the speculative news that diplomatic talks are ongoing, however due to the extreme market volatility, it's hard to make judgement calls on how the war is going, based on Wall Street. From the outside looking in, the financial sector appears confident that the internal power struggles within the White House are an indicator that a return to the war is unlikely this week, as the impact of the midterm elections have complicated the plans that were previously in place.

This may be, however, a false-assumption: the USS Theodore Roosevelt CSG departed Singapore a couple of days ago, and is headed for CENTCOM. In terms of hardware, all of the American assets are still in place, and the past two weeks of striking Iranian observation posts (and possibly also Radar sites) has probably had some effect in terms of shaping fires, or preparatory targeting efforts intended to prepare the battlespace for a wider operation. So far, the only factor that has changed is the rhetoric...there have been zero changes to the force posture throughout the Middle East, and until cargo flights slow down, or assets start moving toward home, all options are still on the table.
