-International Events-

Russia: Overnight, Ukraine conducted an attack at Borisoglebsk Airfield, which resulted in the destruction of roughly 4-6x Russian fighter aircraft. Russia also continued sporadic strikes around Kiev as well as along the southern front with drone strikes targeting infrastructure in the vicinity of Odessa.

Black Sea: Two more commercial vessels were targeted in drone strikes overnight. The M/T *WINE TRADER* (IMO: 8808707) was struck by a drone off the coast of Romania, suffering damage to the bridge as a fire spread throughout the superstructure. A total of 8x crew members remain missing following this strike. Separately, the M/T *YARA J* (IMO: 9221803) was also struck in a similar manner in the same general area.

Strait of Hormuz: Strikes on merchant vessels continue as before. The UKMTO issued two time-late reports of strikes on merchant vessels yesterday, at least one of which involved a large containership which was separate from the M/V *GFS PRIDE* (IMO: 9349045) that sank yesterday afternoon.

Yemen: Last night, Houthi forces claimed to have engaged a Saudi Eurofighter Typhoon aircraft over Saada. Ansar Allah has not claimed to have shot down the aircraft, as the missile was a narrow-miss, and the pilot appears to have survived the detonation of the missile in close proximity to the aircraft.

-HomeFront-

Washington D.C. - Yesterday, the Treasury Department's Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) levied a $1.2 billion fine on Bank of America, after the bank reportedly failed to enforce (or even report) money laundering efforts undertaken in violation of Office of Foreign Assets Control regulations.

Analyst Comment: The Treasury Department stated that this was in relation to a series of money laundering efforts undertaken by nationstate actors (such as China) and cartels in Central America. The exact nature of what kinds of networks were being run has not been disclosed, however Bank of America has already mostly written off the fine as a line item, and no one has been arrested for knowingly using Bank of America resources to help cartels funnel money throughout their organizations.

The intense censorship crackdown due to OPSEC reasons is a fairly significant indicator that something is being planned which requires Operational Security in the first place.
