-International Events-

Persian Gulf: Houthi attacks continued yesterday evening, with missile impacts being reported at the Ras Tanura petroleum hub in Saudi Arabia. Separately, a tanker vessel was struck off the coast of Kuwait yesterday which resulted in unknown damage.

Egypt: On Monday, the Egyptian Parliament voted to authorize the deployment of troops to Saudi Arabia, to provide defensive forces for the Kingdom as Houthi attacks continue to escalate.

Analyst Comment: There has been some speculation that Egypt might seek to get involved with the war on the Houthis, however this might be a bridge too far for the Egyptian military, which has not conducted large scale combat operations outside her borders since 1991. Nevertheless, the total troop deployment will be important to follow, as Saudi Arabia does not have a ground force capable of any major ground offensive in Yemen and the Egyptian Army would be a candidate to supplement whatever the Saudis have planned.

Brazil: Last night the elections concluded with a victory for current incumbent President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva. Flávio Bolsonaro has not conceded defeat, and reportedly escaped the country on a private aircraft this afternoon to avoid being immediately arrested.

Analyst Comment: Brazilian politics are often very high stakes, and in this case Bolsonaro was very likely to be arrested on whatever charges could be written up. Unconfirmed reporting from within the White House indicates that he was possibly extracted more directly by the United States, who had allegedly planned to get him out of the country with US assets in the event that he lost.

Israel: This afternoon the national elections came to a close, with early exit polls indicating no clear winner. As of this report, Eisenkot's faction was barely leading, but the race is neck-and-neck in the hours after the polls closed.

United Kingdom: This week a new series of Respect Orders went into effect, granting authorities more power to restrict the behavior of individuals throughout the nation. These new Respect Orders build on pre-existing Anti-Social Behavior Orders, which ban "behaviour by a person which causes, or is likely to cause, harassment, alarm or distress to persons not of the same household as the person". As such, individuals who may possibly cause someone stress in the future, can now be imprisoned for up to two years.

London: This morning two men appeared in court on charges pertaining to their involvement in the alleged terror plot at RAF Fairford three weeks ago. The accused have been identified as Mohmed Ahmad Abdulkarim and Nouman Essghir, two individuals who were allegedly involved in some manner with the attempt to load three vans with fuel drums in an attempt to conduct some sort of attack.

Analyst Comment: Sometimes a picture is worth a thousand words, and for those seeking to determine whether or not this incident was a false-flag operation, the evidence presented so far during this case has been very illuminating. The lead suspect being wheelchair bound due to a serious medical condition is merely the latest detail that casts doubt as to the validity of rest the investigation, which now borders on the absurd. Four of the other suspects remain at large after being granted bail, and so far no evidence has been presented whatsoever which indicates any attack was planned at all. No explosives were found and the suspects were not located near any gate or base entrance. Nevertheless, the United States has formally taken the position that this was a terror attack, after which the United Kingdom adopted the same position as well.

-HomeFront-

Michigan: Overnight a barricaded shooter situation was reported on Running Deer Ridge in Leroy. Local authorities state that the incident began after the suspect's parents became concerned about their son's behavior, which prompted law enforcement becoming involved last night. Upon arrival, the situation immediately devolved into a standoff, as the suspect remained barricaded within the family residence. During the standoff, a total of two police drones were shot down, which prompted civil aviation at the nearby Battle Creek Executive Airport to halt operations for some time. Eventually, the standoff came to an end after the suspect set the structure on fire from within. Only one fatality was reported (the suspect) who has been identified as 35 year old Travis Baker.

Analyst Comment: Around the world, governments have continued to urge their populations to prepare for calamity, in the event that conflict comes to their various homelands. The United Kingdom has been carrying out their "Be Ready" preparedness campaign since last week, along with similar campaigns in Germany, the Netherlands, Poland, Sweden, and many others. Japan has also rolled out their latest disaster response plan to dramatically increase the stockpiled resources in the event of emergency, however Japan's system is almost entirely constructed around earthquake preparedness and not the warfare/conflict that Europe is expecting.

Due to these efforts taking place around the same time as yesterday's announcement from the Pentagon regarding domestic military preparedness efforts, concerns are growing throughout the western world. To be sure, there is great financial incentive to generate panic buying, which must always be considered as well. At the moment, there is only one major flashpoint that might imminently result in regional instability, which is the potential return to more active combat conditions in the Middle East. However, even if the worst should happen in that case, the impacts within the homeland would not be as apocalyptic as social media might lead people to believe. Rational, proportionate, reasonable, and feasible preparedness efforts are always recommended, but there is currently no specific threat that would warrant panic buying or unplanned readiness measures.
